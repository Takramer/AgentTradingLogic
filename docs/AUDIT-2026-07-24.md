## 1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT

**Status: LIKELY OVERFIT AND NOT DEPLOYABLE. The headline result is an artifact
of accounting, not an edge.**

The reported book (`+6,178R` / `+6,577R` "clean equity book") is **[V]**:

- a **gross, zero-cost** number (forensically confirmed below), in violation of
  the project's own written rule "Always pass `--cost-bps 5`";
- a sum over **114,964 signals of which a real account can take 230–448
  (0.20–0.39%)**;
- generated over **one 10-month window (2025-09-11 → 2026-07-17)** containing a
  single bull regime and one 20-day selloff;
- on a **survivorship-biased universe** selected from the *most recent* 20
  cached sessions;
- with **structural universe decisions (ETF/ADR/biotech exclusion) chosen by
  looking at the full-sample P&L of this exact artifact**.

When the same trade list is run through the repo's own portfolio simulator at
the documented 1% risk, 5 positions, 2 new/day:

| | Return | Max DD | Sharpe | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy (5 pos / 2 per day) | **+6.68%** | **19.94%** | **0.43** | 230 |
| Strategy (10 pos / 4 per day) | +28.27% | 20.95% | 1.05 | 437 |
| **SPY buy-and-hold, same window** | **+13.03%** | **9.13%** | **1.11** | 1 |

The configuration the repo documents **loses to SPY on return, with 2.2× the
drawdown and 39% of the Sharpe.** The looser configuration beats SPY on return
with **identical risk-adjusted performance** and 2.3× the drawdown.

**Can it be salvaged?** The *infrastructure* can — `nulltest.py`, `crossval.py`,
`sanity.py` and the config-driven ablation loop are genuinely good bones, better
than most retail research stacks. The *strategy* cannot: there is no measurable
alpha left after beta, costs, and clustering are removed.

### Three greatest threats to validity

1. **The R-sum is not a portfolio.** 114,964 unbounded, fully-overlapping
   signals are summed as if each were a fundable 1R bet. 99.8% are untakeable.
   Every headline number in the repo inherits this.
2. **Trade-level IID statistics on massively overlapping trades.** 540 signals
   per day across 3,171 names are treated as independent observations. The
   reported `t=+15.69` is `t=+2.87` when clustered by day, and `t=+1.78` once
   market beta is removed.
3. **Full-sample selection on the reported metric.** The ETF, ADR, biotech,
   `min_stop_pct`, `winsor_r_cap`, `legMask` and PEAD decisions were all made by
   observing total R on the same 213 days. Zero multiple-testing control exists
   anywhere in the repo.

### Stop immediately

1. Stop reporting **sum-of-R** or **trade-count-based t-stats** as performance.
2. Stop running anything at `--cost-bps 0` (the current default in
   `backtest.py`, `portfolio.py`, `optimize.py`, `nulltest.py`).
3. Stop making universe/filter decisions by comparing full-sample R totals.
4. Stop treating `is_oos_split` in `sanity.py` as out-of-sample — that window has
   been viewed during every config decision for two weeks.
5. Stop the Monte Carlo in its current form; it is measuring the wrong quantity.

## 2. FALSE-CONFIDENCE MAP

Ranked by severity × effect on reported performance.

### FC-1 · CRITICAL · The R-sum has no capital constraint

- **Evidence [V]:** `rhengine/backtest.py:260-296` emits a trade for every
  (ticker, bar) passing the score with no position, capital, or concurrency
  limit. `tests/results/current_full.csv` = 114,964 trades / 213 sessions /
  3,171 tickers = **540 signals per day (median 514, max 1,319)**.
  `rhengine/portfolio.py` (the only module with constraints) takes **230 of
  114,964 (0.20%)** at the documented 5-position setting.
- **Affects:** every reported R total, `sanity.py`, `montecarlo.py`,
  `mc_refined.py`, `crossval.py`, `ic.py`, all five notebooks.
- **Effect:** Inflates apparent scale ~250–500×. PF and avg-R are computed on a
  population that cannot be traded. Monte Carlo resamples from it.
- **Remediation:** Make `portfolio.simulate_portfolio` the *only* source of
  performance metrics. Delete `summarize()`'s R totals from every report path,
  or relabel them "signal diagnostics — not a book".
- **Proof test:** `test_no_r_sum_in_reports` — assert no reporting function
  returns `sum(r_multiple)`; `test_portfolio_is_metric_source` — assert
  `sanity`/`montecarlo` accept only a `taken` trade list from `portfolio`.

### FC-2 · CRITICAL · Headline artifact is gross of all costs

- **Evidence [V]:** Forensic recovery from trade geometry. For the 41,446 trades
  exiting exactly at the stop, regressing `r_multiple` on `1/stop_pct` gives
  **intercept = −1.0000, slope = −0.000000 → implied round-trip cost = 0.00 bps**.
  `.claude/skills/strat-refine/SKILL.md` mandates "**Always pass `--cost-bps 5`**".
  Defaults are `0.0` in `backtest.py:397`, `portfolio.py:214`, `optimize.py:226`,
  `nulltest.py:213`.
- **Effect [V]:** Cost sensitivity of the book, incremental round-trip bps:

  | +bps | mean R | sum R |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | +0.0572 | +6,577 |
  | 5 | +0.0316 | +3,638 |
  | 10 | +0.0061 | +700 |
  | **11.2** | **0.0000** | **breakeven** |
  | 20 | −0.0450 | −5,176 |
  | 50 | −0.1984 | −22,806 |

- **The universe floor is `min_dollar_volume = 500,000`** (`config.json:73`) —
  a $500k/day median-ADV name. Effective round-trip cost in that liquidity tier
  is routinely 30–100+ bps (half-spread × 2 + impact), not 11.
- **Remediation:** Change every `--cost-bps` default to a *distribution*, not a
  scalar: model cost as `f(ADV, price, spread proxy)`. Minimum interim step —
  default `--cost-bps 30` and require an explicit flag to go lower.
- **Proof test:** `test_cost_default_nonzero` — assert every argparse
  `--cost-bps` default > 0. `test_cost_model_scales_with_adv` — assert a
  $500k-ADV name is charged ≥3× a $500M-ADV name.

### FC-3 · CRITICAL · Trade-level IID statistics on overlapping trades

- **Evidence [V]:** `backtest.py:330-334` and `sanity.py:39-45` compute
  `t = mean / (std/sqrt(n))` with `n` = trade count. With 540 concurrent signals
  per day, trades are cross-sectionally near-identical (same market factor).
  - naive t (n=114,964): **+15.69**
  - day-clustered t (equal-weight daily basket, n=213): **+2.87**
  - **inflation factor 5.5×**
- **Affects:** `backtest._stats`, `sanity.check_t_stat`, `optimize` pooled OOS
  line, `crossval` fold stats, `ic.py`, all notebooks.
- **Effect:** Every significance claim in the repo is overstated ~5.5×.
  `sanity.py` returns `[PASS] t_stat t=+15.69` on a book whose honest t is 2.87
  before beta adjustment and 1.78 after.
- **Remediation:** Replace `_stats` t-stat with a **daily-clustered** or
  Newey-West-adjusted statistic computed on the daily portfolio return series.
  Report `n_effective = number of trading days`, not trade count.
- **Proof test:** `test_tstat_uses_daily_clustering` — feed 1,000 duplicate
  trades on one date; assert reported t does not increase with duplication.

### FC-4 · CRITICAL · Universe is survivorship-biased and look-ahead selected

- **Evidence [V]:** `data_local.py:289-328` — `massive_universe()` builds the
  ticker list from the **most recent `lookback_sessions=20` cached days**, then
  applies that fixed list to the entire historical window. Any name delisted,
  acquired, bankrupted, or fallen below $1 / $500k ADV before the last 20
  sessions is absent from the whole backtest.
  Price/volume floors (`statistics.median(cs) < min_price`,
  `median(dollars[t]) < min_dollar_vol`) are computed on **today's** 20 days.
- **Compounding [V]:** `exclude_etfs`, `exclude_adr`, `exclude_biotech` use
  **present-day** classification files (`etf_blocklist.json` refreshed from the
  Nasdaq directory today; `sector_map.json` with **current** market cap).
  `biotech_smallcap_max_mktcap` decides whether a 2025 trade was in a "small-cap"
  using its 2026 market cap.
- **Effect:** Unquantifiable upward bias, but directionally large — the excluded
  population is exactly the losers. `config.json:76` states the ETF rule alone
  moved the book from **−1,712R to +6,178R**; that is the entire reported edge,
  produced by a filter chosen from full-sample results.
- **Remediation:** Build a **point-in-time universe**: for each rebalance date,
  select from names tradeable *as of that date* using only prior-20-session
  price/volume. Snapshot ETF/sector/mktcap classification with a `valid_from`
  date and join as-of.
- **Proof test:** `test_universe_is_point_in_time` — assert the universe for
  date D is identical whether the cache ends at D or at D+250 sessions.
  This test **fails today**.

### FC-5 · CRITICAL · Edge lives in the top 1–2% of trades and 10 days

- **Evidence [V]:**

  | cut | mean R |
  |---|---|
  | base | +0.0572 |
  | drop top 0.5% | +0.0294 |
  | drop top 1% | +0.0117 |
  | **drop top 2%** | **−0.0174** |
  | drop top 5% | −0.0896 |

  Top 1,000 trades (0.87%) = **72.4% of total R**.
  **Top 10 sessions = 107% of total R** (the other 203 days net negative).
  4 of 11 months are negative (2025-10: −2,621R; 2026-02: −1,301R).
- **Effect:** IID bootstrap on this distribution is meaningless — the mean is set
  by a handful of clustered days, not by a repeatable per-trade expectancy.
- **Remediation:** Report *day*-level and *month*-level net contribution, not
  gross-win share. Fix `sanity.check_month_concentration` /
  `check_ticker_concentration`, which use **gross wins only** (`sanity.py:146-161`)
  and therefore cannot see a losing month at all.
- **Proof test:** `test_month_concentration_uses_net` — a book with one +1000R
  month and ten −50R months must not PASS.

### FC-6 · HIGH · The "edge" is largely market beta

- **Evidence [V]:** Regress the equal-weight daily signal basket R on SPY's
  forward 5-day return (median hold ≈ 5 sessions), n = 208 days:
  - `corr(basket R, SPY fwd-5d) = +0.478`
  - raw mean daily R **+0.0728** → market-neutral intercept **+0.0402**
    (**45% of the edge is beta**)
  - **t-stat of the alpha intercept = +1.78** (below the conventional 2.0, before
    any multiple-testing haircut)
  - 8 of the top 10 R days precede SPY 5-day moves of +1.7% to +5.4%
- **Effect:** The system is a levered, long-only, high-turnover way to buy US
  equity beta before rebounds. No benchmark comparison exists anywhere in the
  repo (`grep benchmark|buy_and_hold` → no hits in `rhengine/`), so this was
  never visible.
- **Remediation:** Add exposure-matched benchmarking to every report: strategy
  vs SPY, vs beta-matched SPY, vs equal-weight universe. Report alpha with its
  own standard error.
- **Proof test:** `test_report_includes_benchmark` — assert every experiment
  report contains `alpha`, `beta`, `benchmark_return`, `alpha_tstat`.

### FC-7 · HIGH · Selection rule adds nothing over random

- **Evidence [V]:** Replaced `score` / `weighted_score` with uniform random
  values (destroying the ranking) and re-ran `portfolio.simulate_portfolio`
  across 200 seeds at 10 positions / 4 per day:

  | | p5 | p25 | **p50** | p75 | p95 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Random selection | −17.2% | +7.7% | **+26.0%** | +44.8% | +71.6% |

  Score-ranked selection returned **+28.27% — the 53rd percentile of random.**
- **Corroborating [V]:** `sanity.py` already reports
  `[WARN] score_monotonicity 3: +0.182 (n=5592) | 4: +0.051 (n=109372)` — the
  **higher** score tier earns **less**. The ranking is inverted.
- **Effect:** The entire spread from −17% to +72% is *which* of 540 daily signals
  you happen to take. That is the dominant risk factor in the system, and it is
  not modelled anywhere.
- **Remediation:** Either find a ranking variable with demonstrated cross-sectional
  IC, or accept random selection and size for the resulting dispersion. Do not
  claim conviction tiers work.
- **Proof test:** `test_ranking_beats_random` — score-ranked portfolio must
  exceed the 80th percentile of 200 random-selection runs. **Fails today (53rd).**

### FC-8 · HIGH · Residual momentum is a units error against its own citation

- **Evidence [V]:** `indicators.py:711-743`. `reg_n = min(L, 36)` — beta/alpha
  estimated on **36 daily** returns. `SKIP, FORM = 1, 12` — formation window is
  **12 daily** returns with a **1-day** skip.
  The cited source (`indicators.py:830-831`, "Blitz/Huij/Martens sort on exactly
  this") uses a **36-month** estimation window and **12-month** formation with a
  **1-month** skip ([Blitz, Huij & Martens, *Residual Momentum*, J. Empirical
  Finance 18(3), 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2011.01.003)).
- The implementation has transposed **months → days**. At a 12-day horizon the
  documented US equity effect is **short-term reversal**, not momentum
  ([Jegadeesh 1990](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1990.tb05110.x);
  [Lehmann 1990](https://doi.org/10.2307/2937816)). The economic hypothesis
  claimed for this sleeve does not apply to the horizon implemented.
- **Effect:** The sleeve carrying **94% of the book** has no literature support
  at the horizon it trades. Its day-clustered t is **+1.32** (below noise).
- **Remediation:** Either (a) re-implement at the cited horizon (36-month beta,
  12-month formation, 1-month skip, monthly rebalance) — a genuinely different,
  far lower-turnover strategy; or (b) drop the residual-momentum framing and
  test it honestly as a 12-day risk-adjusted-residual signal against a
  short-term-reversal null.
- **Proof test:** `test_residmom_horizon_matches_spec` — assert
  `FORM`/`SKIP`/`reg_n` are read from config and documented in trading days with
  an explicit citation to the horizon actually tested.

### FC-9 · HIGH · Stock/SPY return series aligned by position, not date

- **Evidence [V]:** `indicators.py:715-720` — `stock_slice = bars[-N:]`,
  `spy_slice = spy_bars[-N:]`. Aligned by **index from the end**, never by date.
  Bars with any `None` OHLC are dropped upstream (`data_local.py:125-126`),
  creating per-ticker gaps.
  Measured on the last 57 non-empty cached sessions: **2,450 of 13,373 tickers
  (18.3%) have at least one missing session** and therefore regress against
  date-misaligned SPY returns.
- **Effect:** Beta and residuals are corrupted for ~18% of the universe —
  concentrated in exactly the thin, $500k-ADV names that dominate by count.
- **Remediation:** Join stock and SPY returns on **date** (inner join on a
  shared calendar) before regressing. Reject any ticker with <90% coverage in
  the estimation window.
- **Proof test:** `test_residmom_date_aligned` — insert a synthetic ticker with
  5 missing sessions; assert its beta equals the beta computed from a
  date-joined series.

### FC-10 · HIGH · Monte Carlo resamples IID from an untradeable population

- **Evidence [V]:** `montecarlo.py:47` `rng.choice(rs)`; `mc_refined.py:144`
  `rng.choice(rs, size=(paths, n_trades))`. Both are IID-with-replacement over
  per-trade R. `grep block|stationary|circular` in both files → **no hits**.
  Input is the full 114,964-trade list, not the ~400 takeable ones.
- **Effect:** Destroys the day-clustering that produces real drawdowns (203 of
  213 days net negative; losses arrive together). **Understates drawdown and
  tail risk.** Simultaneously it reports dispersion driven by fat-tailed
  individual R rather than by the real dominant risk — selection luck (FC-7,
  p5 −17% to p95 +72%).
- **Remediation:** See §6. Resample **days**, in blocks, from the
  capital-constrained portfolio return series.
- **Proof test:** `test_mc_preserves_clustering` — assert simulated max-drawdown
  p95 ≥ the realised historical max drawdown. Currently not guaranteed.

### FC-11 · MEDIUM · Two non-equivalent backtest engines; MC runs the wrong one

- **Evidence [V]:** `backtest.simulate_long` (event loop, pessimistic stop-first
  ordering, chandelier trail) vs `ablate_vbt` / `mc_refined.harvest_refined_trades`
  (vectorbt `Portfolio.from_signals`, different stop precedence, signal-shifted
  time exits at `mc_refined.py:102`, no chandelier).
  `mc_refined.REFINED_SET` (`mc_refined.py:38-44`) trades **residualmomentum,
  macd, bollinger, vwap, pead**. The deployed `config.json` routes only
  **residualmomentum + bollinger** (`indicators.py:1037-1041`), and
  `pead_always_on = false`.
  `tests/results/refined_trades.csv` covers **2024-10-15 → 2026-07-17** (244,239
  trades); `current_full.csv` covers **2025-09-11 → 2026-07-17** (114,964).
- **Effect:** The Monte Carlo deliverable (`notebooks/montecarlo_refined.ipynb`)
  describes a **different strategy set, on a different window, from a different
  simulator** than the one `config.json` deploys. Its percentiles do not
  describe the live system.
- **Remediation:** One engine. Keep the event loop (it is the live-representative
  one); use vectorbt only for parameter sweeps, gated by a cross-engine agreement
  test.
- **Proof test:** `test_engines_agree` — on a 20-ticker fixture, assert
  event-loop and vbt trade lists match on entry date, exit date and R within
  tolerance. **This test does not exist.**

### FC-12 · MEDIUM · Walk-forward has no purge or embargo

- **Evidence [V]:** `optimize.walk_forward` (`optimize.py:165-212`) splits on
  `signal_date` only. Trades hold up to `max_hold_sessions = 10`, so signals
  fired just before a boundary resolve inside the test fold.
  `crossval.py` *does* implement `--embargo-days 15`; `optimize.py` does not —
  and `optimize.py`'s docstring calls its output "the honest promotion number"
  (`crossval.py:4-5`).
- **Effect:** With 213 signal days and 4 folds (~53 days each), a 10-session
  overlap contaminates ~19% of each test fold.
- **Remediation:** Move the embargo logic from `crossval._shift` into a shared
  `splits.py`; apply to every splitter.
- **Proof test:** `test_walkforward_purges_boundary` — assert no trade in a test
  fold has `signal_date < fold_start` **and** exit inside the fold.

### FC-13 · MEDIUM · Realized-only equity marking hides open drawdown

- **Evidence [V]:** `portfolio.py:88-95` — equity changes only on the exit
  session. Docstring (`portfolio.py:17-18`) claims this is "conservative for
  intraday drawdown". It is **anti-conservative**: open losing positions are
  invisible until they close, so peak-to-trough of the realized curve cannot
  exceed that of the true mark-to-market curve.
- **Effect:** The reported 19.94% / 20.95% max drawdowns are **floors, not
  estimates**. Sharpe computed off this series (`portfolio.py:150-162`) is not
  the Sharpe of an investable account; the direction of that bias is
  indeterminate without building the MTM curve.
- **Remediation:** Mark open positions daily against bar closes. Report both
  realized and MTM curves.
- **Proof test:** `test_mtm_dd_ge_realized_dd` — assert MTM max drawdown ≥
  realized max drawdown on any book with open positions.

### FC-14 · MEDIUM · Untracked trial count, no multiple-testing control

- **Evidence [V]:** `grep -rn "PBO|deflated|probabilistic_sharpe|bonferroni|
  benjamini|reality_check"` across all `.py` and `.ipynb` → **zero hits**.
  Trials documented *in `config.json` comments alone*: ETF exclusion,
  ADR exclusion, biotech exclusion, `plain_symbols_only`, `min_stop_pct`
  (0.003 → 0.0075), `winsor_r_cap` on/off, `pead_always_on` on/off,
  `legMask` for residualmomentum, `legMask` for macd. **≥9 structural trials on
  one 213-day sample**, plus prior rejections (RSI-2 mean reversion, VIX gate,
  vix_utils term gate) recorded outside the repo. No experiment ledger exists.
- **Effect:** With ≥12 trials, a Bonferroni-style threshold is t ≈ 2.9–3.1.
  The observed post-beta alpha t is **1.78**. The system does not clear its own
  multiple-testing burden.
- **Remediation:** Ledger every trial (§8 Phase 0). Compute Deflated Sharpe
  ([Bailey & López de Prado 2014](https://doi.org/10.3905/jpm.2014.40.5.094))
  and PBO ([Bailey et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697688.2016.1180988))
  with the true trial count.
- **Proof test:** `test_ledger_nonempty_before_promotion` — promotion CLI exits
  non-zero if `experiments.jsonl` has fewer entries than distinct configs in git
  history.

### FC-15 · MEDIUM · `sanity.py` thresholds are calibrated to pass

- **Evidence [V]:** Actual output on the headline artifact:
  `[PASS] cost_stress avgR +0bps +0.0572 | +5 +0.0316 | +10 +0.0061`.
  The check stops at +10 bps — where **89% of the edge is already gone** and
  breakeven is 11.2 bps. It PASSES one bp before death.
  `[PASS] ticker_concentration EVC = 0.1% of gross wins` — with 3,171 tickers no
  single name can reach the 15% threshold; the check is structurally incapable
  of failing.
  `[PASS] sample_size n=114964` — measures trade count, not effective sample
  (213 days).
- **Remediation:** cost_stress at +25/+50/+100 bps; concentration on **top-20
  aggregate net** contribution; sample_size on **distinct sessions**.
- **Proof test:** `test_sanity_fails_known_bad_book` — a synthetic book whose
  edge is entirely in 5 days must FAIL at least three checks.

## 6. MONTE CARLO REDESIGN

### 6.1 Current flaws

1. **Wrong population.** Resamples 114,964 signals; ~400 are takeable (FC-1).
2. **Wrong unit.** Resamples *trades*; the correlated unit is the *day*.
3. **IID assumption untested.** 540 same-day trades share one market factor;
   `corr(basket, SPY fwd-5d) = +0.478`. Losses cluster: 203 of 213 days are net
   negative and 10 days carry 107% of R.
4. **Understates drawdown.** Independent draws cannot reproduce the
   simultaneous stop-outs that generate real drawdowns.
5. **Misses the dominant risk.** Selection luck spans p5 −17% to p95 +72%
   (FC-7). Not modelled at all.
6. **Sequential compounding of concurrent positions.** `eq *= 1 + f*R` treats up
   to 10 simultaneous positions as sequential bets on a growing base.

### 6.2 Why the spread is wide

Ranked by contribution, from this audit's measurements:

1. **Selection luck** — which 2–4 of ~540 daily signals get taken. Dominant.
2. **Weak edge** — post-beta alpha t = 1.78; the mean is barely distinguishable
   from zero, so terminal dispersion swamps drift.
3. **Concentration** — top 2% of trades flip the sign; 10 days carry the book.
4. **Cost fragility** — 11 bps of assumption moves the book from +6,577R to 0.
5. **Fat tails** — SL mean −1.045R with uncapped downside, upside winsorized
   at +10R.

It is **not** primarily "unrealistic simulation design" — but the current design
is wide for the *wrong* reasons and simultaneously too *narrow* on drawdown.

### 6.3 Recommended hierarchy

Run in order; each is a gate.

**Level 0 — deterministic baseline.** Single MTM portfolio equity curve from
`portfolio/book.py` at 30 bps. Report realized *and* MTM drawdown.

**Level 1 — selection bootstrap (new, highest value).** Hold signals fixed;
randomize which qualify for the slot 500×. This isolates FC-7. Report the full
percentile fan. If the score-ranked path is not above the 80th percentile, the
ranking is not real.

**Level 2 — stationary block bootstrap on daily book returns.** Resample the
*daily portfolio return series* in blocks; expected block length ≈ 10 sessions
(the max hold), geometric block lengths per
[Politis & Romano (1994)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1994.10476870).
Preserves volatility clustering and loss clustering that IID destroys.

**Level 3 — regime-conditioned block bootstrap.** Sample blocks within
SPY-regime buckets, then reweight to a target regime mix. Prevents a 10-month
bull sample from implying bull-only forward odds.

**Level 4 — cost and execution perturbation.** Round-trip cost drawn per trade
from a distribution keyed to ADV; entry delayed 0/1/2 bars at random; 5% of
orders rejected. Compound with Level 2.

**Level 5 — parameter perturbation.** `minResidualRA`, `stop_atr_mult`,
`target_atr_mult`, `max_hold_sessions` jittered ±20% per path.

### 6.4 Report (all levels)

P5/P25/P50/P75/P95 terminal equity · P(loss) · P(underperform SPY) ·
E[max DD] and its p95/p99 · drawdown duration p50/p95 · risk of ruin at 20%/35%/50% ·
distribution of net PF, Sharpe, alpha · CVaR@5% of terminal wealth ·
sensitivity of P50 to each of cost, delay, parameter jitter.

### 6.5 Interpretation rules

- Monte Carlo is **not** out-of-sample evidence. It re-describes one sample.
- A tight distribution around a positive P50 from an overfit backtest is
  *more* dangerous than a wide one, not less.
- **Never** narrow the distribution by weakening cost, delay, or regime
  assumptions. Only these narrow it legitimately: more independent bets, lower
  concentration, lower cost drag, a genuinely stronger signal.
- If P(underperform SPY) > 40% at Level 3, the strategy has no reason to exist.

### 6.6 Implementation plan

1. `rhengine/validation/mc.py`: `selection_bootstrap()`, `block_bootstrap()`,
   `regime_block_bootstrap()`, `cost_perturb()`, `param_perturb()` — all taking
   a **daily book return series**, not a trade list.
2. Delete `mc_refined.py`. Reduce `montecarlo.py` to a thin CLI over the new
   module.
3. `tests/test_mc.py`: `test_block_bootstrap_preserves_autocorr` (lag-1 autocorr
   within 0.05 of empirical), `test_mc_dd_p95_ge_realized_dd`,

## 7. RESEARCH-BACKED IMPROVEMENT OPPORTUNITIES

Only four. Complexity is penalized; nothing here is added before §8 Phase 1 is
complete.

### O-1 · Fix the horizon of the existing residual-momentum sleeve (P0)

- **Rationale:** The sleeve already implements the right *mathematics* at the
  wrong *frequency* (FC-8). Blitz/Huij/Martens report residual momentum with
  roughly double the Sharpe of total-return momentum at 36-month/12-month/1-month
  ([2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2011.01.003)). The repo's 523-session
  cache (2024-07-18 →) supports a 12-month formation with ~9 months of testable
  signal — thin, but honest.
- **Path:** parameterize `reg_n`, `FORM`, `SKIP` in trading days; test
  (252, 252, 21) against the current (36, 12, 1).
- **Expected impact:** Turnover collapses by ~20×, which by itself moves the
  breakeven cost from 11 bps to ~200 bps — the single largest available
  improvement to net PF.
- **Kill criteria:** if the monthly-rebalanced version has day-clustered alpha
  t < 1.5 net of 30 bps on the 523-session cache, drop residual momentum entirely.
- **Overfit risk:** Low — the parameters come from published literature, not from
  this sample.

### O-2 · Replace scalar cost with an ADV-scaled cost model (P0)

- **Rationale:** The system's survival is determined entirely by an assumption
  currently set to zero. Effective spread scales inversely with liquidity; the
  standard practitioner reference is
  [Corwin & Schultz (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2012.01729.x),
  whose high–low spread estimator can be computed from the OHLC bars **already in
  the cache** — no new data required.
- **Path:** `execution/costs.py`: `cost_bps(ticker, date) = corwin_schultz_spread
  + impact(order_size / ADV)`. Wire into `simulate_long`.
- **Expected impact:** Removes FC-2 permanently and reprices every past result.
- **Kill criteria:** none — this is infrastructure, not a strategy.

### O-3 · Raise the liquidity floor and test capacity (P1)

- **Rationale:** `min_dollar_volume = 500,000` admits names where 30–100 bps
  round-trip is normal. At $10M ADV, 5–15 bps is realistic — inside the 11 bps
  breakeven.
- **Path:** sweep `min_dollar_volume` ∈ {0.5M, 5M, 25M, 100M} **on the point-in-time
  universe**, reporting net portfolio return at ADV-scaled cost.
- **Expected impact:** Fewer, more tradeable signals. Trade count falls from 540/day
  to perhaps 50/day — which also shrinks the selection-luck variance in FC-7.
- **Kill criteria:** if net alpha is negative at every floor, no liquidity tier
  saves it.

### O-4 · Add one genuinely uncorrelated input — but only after Phase 1 (P2)

- **Rationale:** The user's instinct that technical-only is insufficient is
  **correct and confirmed** here: `corr(basket, SPY fwd-5d) = +0.478` and all
  candidate sleeves are long-equity daily-bar signals. Adding a sixth technical
  indicator cannot diversify what is structurally one beta bet.
- **Highest-justified family:** cross-sectional **quality/profitability**
  ([Novy-Marx 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2013.01.003)) or
  **low-volatility** exposure as a *portfolio tilt*, not a timing signal —
  both are slow-moving, survive the repo's daily-bar data, and are negatively
  correlated with the current beta-chasing profile.
- **Data requirement:** point-in-time fundamentals with `valid_from`. The
  existing Finnhub/AlphaVantage adapters are **not** currently point-in-time
  **[A]** — that must be established first.
- **Kill criteria:** if the addition does not lower portfolio-level max drawdown
  by ≥20% at equal return, reject it.

**Explicitly rejected:** more technical indicators, more sleeves, dynamic sleeve
allocation, ML signal combination, options overlays (see §10).

1. **The system is not edgeless at realistic cost — it is significantly and

## 12e. O-1 RESULT — THE LITERATURE HORIZON DOES NOT RESCUE THE SLEEVE

**Verdict: O-1 FAILS its predeclared kill criterion. Residual momentum is dead
at every horizon tested. My stated mechanism for why it might work was wrong.**

Reproduce: `py scripts/o1_horizon_test.py --max-tickers 120`

Data: `yahoo_cache` deep history, SPY refetched to 2,960 bars. Signal window
2018-11-15 → 2026-07-24 — **1,930 sessions, 7.7 years**, spanning the 2018
selloff, COVID, the 2022 bear market and 2025-26. SPY buy-and-hold **+204.3%**.
Universe is 120 current-membership S&P500/NDX names, so it is
survivorship-biased and **optimistic in absolute terms**; both arms carry the
identical bias, so the horizon comparison is valid.

Selectivity was matched first: `minResidualRA` is compared against
`mean(residuals)/std(residuals)`, whose dispersion scales as `1/sqrt(formBars)`.
Measured std 0.3315 at formBars=12 vs 0.0650 at 252 (ratio 5.10; theory
`sqrt(252/12)`=4.58). At the same nominal 0.15 the short arm admits 52.8% of
observations and the long arm 1.0%. The long arms therefore run at the
scale-equivalent 0.03. An earlier run that skipped this produced 14 trades and
was a scaling artifact, not a result.

| arm | signals/yr | avg hold | mean R @0 | **t @0** | mean R @30 | **t @30** | breakeven | portfolio @5 bps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SHORT 36/12/1, ATR exits | 6,572 | 6.5 | +0.1100 | **+7.67** | −0.0729 | **−5.48** | **18.0 bps** | +173.2% |
| LONG 756/252/21, ATR exits | 263 | 7.5 | **+0.1287** | +2.23 | −0.0647 | −0.44 | **20.0 bps** | +47.4% |
| LONG 756/252/21, wide stops | 268 | 13.8 | +0.0365 | +1.98 | −0.0098 | −0.02 | **23.7 bps** | +7.8% |
| SPY buy-and-hold | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | **+204.3%** |

### What was right

**The long horizon has a better per-trade edge**: +0.1287 vs +0.1100 mean R at
zero cost. The premise that the sleeve was measuring the wrong horizon was not
unfounded — at the literature horizon each trade is slightly better.

**Its t-statistic is more trustworthy than it looks.** +2.23 on 93 monthly
rebalance dates is computed on near-independent observations. The short arm's
+7.67 is computed on ~1,930 daily dates with heavily overlapping holds and is
inflated by exactly the clustering described in FC-3. A lower t on honest
observations is not obviously worse evidence.

### What was wrong — my mechanism

I predicted monthly rebalancing would cut turnover ~20x and move required cost
from ~5.5 bps to ~110 bps. **Turnover did fall 25x (6,572 → 263 signals/yr).
Cost tolerance moved from 18.0 to 20.0 bps.** The prediction was off by an order
of magnitude, for a reason that is worth recording:

**Cost measured in R is `2 * bps / stop_pct`. It depends on the STOP DISTANCE,
not on how often you trade.** Cutting trade frequency reduces how many times you
pay, but every trade still pays the same fraction of its own R. Per-trade
expectancy is therefore almost unaffected by turnover. My reasoning silently
conflated *total* cost drag with *per-trade* cost drag.

The one arm that did improve cost tolerance materially (23.7 bps) got there by
**widening the stop** (6 ATR vs 1.5), not by holding longer — larger `stop_pct`
directly shrinks cost in R. Its cost sensitivity is 4x lower per bp
(slope 0.00154 vs 0.00610 mean R per bp). But its edge is also 3x smaller
(+0.0365 vs +0.1100), so the net gain in breakeven is only ~1.3x.

**If there is any remaining lever on cost tolerance in this codebase, it is stop
geometry, not signal horizon.** That is a much narrower and less promising
thread than O-1 was.

### Kill criterion

Predeclared: *"day-clustered alpha t < 1.5 net of 30 bps and residual momentum
is dead."* Results at 30 bps: SHORT **−5.48**, LONG ATR **−0.44**, LONG wide
**−0.02**. **All three fail. Residual momentum is retired at every horizon.**

Note also that every arm loses to SPY at every non-zero cost level. The short
arm beats SPY only at exactly 0 bps (+622.9% vs +204.3%), with 49.1% max
drawdown, and is −98.9% at 30 bps. The long arms never approach SPY because they
are flat most of the time — 371 taken trades across 7.7 years.

### Consequence

Sleeve scorecard §4.1 changes from **REBUILD** to **REMOVE**. There is no
horizon at which this signal pays for its own transaction costs. Nothing in the
remaining backlog has a stronger prior than this one did, so **the recommendation
is to stop adding and stop testing variants of this sleeve.**

## 12d. OPEN ACTION — THE NYDEX JS PORT CARRIES THE SAME BUG

**Status: unfixed, outside this repo. Owner: whoever maintains the JS engine.**

`fixtures/golden.json` was regenerated on 2026-07-24 (commit `91176db`) after the
FC-9 date-alignment fix. `fixtures/bars.json` and `fixtures/adapters.json` are
byte-identical (sha256 unchanged); only `golden.json` moved, by 4 value lines in
2 of 6 cases:

| fixture | bars | change |
|---|---|---|
| `uptrend`, `downtrend`, `choppy_reversal` | 80, aligned | **unchanged** |
| `squeeze_breakout` | 79 | RA-Res −0.371 → −0.297, **beta +0.09 → −0.13** |
| `earnings_gap_drift` | 77 | RA-Res 0.335 → 0.334, beta +0.02 → +0.08 |

No leg `pass` flag changed, so no trade decision flips in the fixtures.

These vectors exist to detect cross-runtime drift with the Nydex JS port. The JS
implementation pairs stock and SPY returns **by position**, exactly as the Python
did, and will now fail these vectors. **That divergence is intentional and the JS
side is the incorrect one.** Until it applies the same fix:

1. Any signal the JS engine produces for a ticker whose bar series does not
   exactly match SPY's sessions estimates beta against the market's *future*
   returns.
2. Python and JS will disagree on ~18% of the universe.

Fix in JS: pair returns on the bar timestamp before regressing, i.e. build a
`{time: close}` map from the SPY series and select only stock bars present in it,
then difference the matched arrays. Mirror of `indicators.py:score_residual_momentum`
under `alignByDate`.

Do not "fix" the drift by reverting the Python or regenerating fixtures from the
JS output. That would reinstate the look-ahead.
